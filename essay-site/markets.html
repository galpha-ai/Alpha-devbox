The Public-Markets Map

Each technical thesis mapped to where value accrues — expressed through listed companies

Companion to the roadmap. Public-tradable focus; tickers throughout and consolidated in the final table. This is research analysis, not investment advice — names are exposure vectors, not recommendations.

The technology dictates the capital structure. If the model layer converges and open-sources, there is no moat in the brain — the value migrates to the two ends of the barbell: the data/verification that feeds the recipe, and the actuator supply-chain choke points that no amount of software can conjure. This is the map of those two ends.

0. The framework: a barbell, not a home run

The technical conclusions force the structure. Model architectures converge and open-source — no durable moat at the model layer. Integrators then get squeezed from both sides — component pricing power below, software/data rent above (the Android-OEM margin script). Therefore, build a barbell:

  • One end — data & verification infrastructure: the recipe's fuel and its examiner — ego capture, auto-labeling pipelines, force/tactile sensing, machine-vision QC.
  • Other end — actuator supply-chain choke points: precision reducers, planetary roller screws, magnets, and the grinding machines behind them — precision-bound, slow to expand, concentrated.
  • The middle — integrators — only by exception, when a fleet is provably a data asset.

Two structural notes before the mapping:

  1. Timing convexity is asymmetric across the barbell. Component names already have non-robot revenue (industrial robots, EVs, machine tools) and get re-rated by humanoid volume; data/verification names are earlier and more binary.
  2. Most of the pure-play excellence is listed in Shanghai / Shenzhen / Tokyo, not New York. The U.S.-listed expression of this thesis is mostly indirect — hyperscalers, semis, sensors.

1. Video hours scale the planner and the judge → corpus owners & compute

If a million hours of egocentric video buys the planner, the trajectory prior, and the reward model, then the scarce assets are (a) the corpus, (b) the capture device, (c) training/inference compute.

  • Alphabet (GOOGL) — the single most complete vertical stack on earth for this thesis: YouTube (the found-data corpus), Genie/Veo (world models), Gemini Robotics + the RT-X lineage (planner), TPUs (compute). The market prices none of it as a robotics-data position. The "Reddit moment" for video licensing is a live catalyst: when video corpora start trading like text corpora did in 2023–24, YouTube is the reserve currency.
  • Meta (META) — the two-sided expression: Reels as latent corpus, and Aria / smart glasses as the ego-capture instrument. The production finding that one Aria-hour beats one teleop-hour makes the glasses platform a robotics-data asset, not a consumer-gadget bet.
  • Apple (AAPL) — Vision Pro's hand tracking already produced EgoDex; the device is a per-frame SE(3) hand-pose recorder as exhaust. Apple's privacy posture makes it a dataset publisher rather than a data merchant; the optionality is in devices (glasses) and on-device models, not data sales.
  • NVIDIA (NVDA) (first of three appearances) — GR00T + Cosmos + Isaac is a bid to own the planner and the gym; ego-scale pretraining is GPU demand of a new flavor.

Falsifier / monitor: the ego-pretraining sample-efficiency exponent (α) decaying → de-rate the corpus thesis; the first paid video-licensing deal for robot training → re-rate it.

2. Force is the scarce label; the middle layer is force-grounding → sensing

The pyramid's only force-observing layer is instrumented interaction. Force/torque and tactile sensing therefore get double-counted — once as data infrastructure (the label source), once as hardware content (per-robot BOM).

  • Six-axis F/T sensors — calibration-intensive, low-automation, concentrated supply; a top-risk / high-barrier node. Public pure-plays are scarce: ATI is private under Novanta (NOVT) — the cleanest listed route.
  • Tactile ("electronic skin") — a category-creating gap: the largest option value on the entire map and the least mature. Public exposure is mostly indirect today (sensor-material and MEMS suppliers); watch for the first listed pure-play or a major's acquisition as the category legitimizer.
  • Encoders / metrology — Renishaw (RSW.L): precision-metrology content per joint rises with force control (dual-encoder architectures).

Monitor: the emergence of a market price for force-labeled data hours — the moment force labels trade at a premium, this layer re-rates.

3. The planner–cerebellum split; the interface is the new OS → edge compute & the logging tail

  • NVIDIA (NVDA) (second appearance) — Jetson Thor is the default onboard brain; the S2→S1 interface standard (GR00T's ambition) is the "CUDA position" of robotics.
  • Qualcomm (QCOM) — the power-constrained second source for onboard inference.
  • The logging tail — an unpriced cross-link. A deployed robot is a data recorder — multi-camera + tactile + joint streams. Fleet logging is a new demand curve for NAND: SanDisk (SNDK) and Kioxia (285A.T) carry a robotics-side argument the market has not modeled.
  • Analog content per joint compounds — gate drivers, current sense, FOC MCUs across ~28+ actuators per humanoid, each a small analog subsystem: Texas Instruments (TXN), Analog Devices (ADI), Infineon (IFX.DE), Monolithic Power (MPWR).

4. The world model is the gym; training environments collect rent → the AWS of robot RL

  • NVIDIA (NVDA) (third appearance) — Isaac/Newton (physics), Omniverse (scenes), Cosmos (neural world models): a near-monopoly on the training environment. Every cerebellum's every training run pays rent here; robot RL consumes GPUs in a different but equally gluttonous pattern than LLM training.

Falsifier: open-weight world models reaching closed-source contact fidelity → the rent compresses, and value flows back to private real data (§1–2).

5. Actuators are this cycle's HBM → reducers, screws, magnets, machines

The hardest-evidence layer: actuators ≈ 56% of core component cost; reducers 30–50% of actuator cost; precision-bound capacity meeting step-function demand.

  • Harmonic reducers — Leaderdrive / 绿的谐波 (688017.SS): >60% China share, >35% global, Tesla-qualified, at 40–60% of Japanese pricing — the classic squeeze structure (grinding-bound supply, steep demand). Harmonic Drive Systems (6324.T) — the precision/brand incumbent; slower to expand, higher ASP. Shuanghuan Transmission (002472.SZ) for planetary/RV.
  • Planetary roller screws — the single most valuable part on the robot (\$1,350–2,700 × ~14 units on Optimus ≈ 19% of cost; <3 µm thread error). Wuzhou New Spring (603667.SS) (~half European pricing), Hengli Hydraulic (601100.SS) (heavy-capex entry), Xinjian Transmission (pre-IPO — watch the listing), with Hiwin (2049.TW), THK (6481.T), NSK (6471.T), Schaeffler / SKF (SKF-B.ST) as the incumbent screw/guide complex.
  • Magnets & rare earths — JL MAG (300748.SZ / 6680.HK), Zhongke Sanhuan (000970.SZ); ex-China strategic premium: MP Materials (MP), Lynas (LYC.AX), Energy Fuels (UUUU), Neo Performance (NEO.TO). The November 10, 2026 cliff is the event catalyst; both branches (extension vs re-tightening) favor ex-China capacity — only the slope differs.
  • The meta choke point — grinding machines. Flexsplines and screws are ground on Swiss/Japanese precision grinders; "the machines that make the machines" are nearly unpriced as a robotics theme. Listed routes: the Japanese machine-tool / abrasives complex — DMG Mori (6141.T), Okuma (6103.T), Noritake (5331.T), Disco (6146.T) adjacents; United Grinding is private.
  • Modules — Sanhua (002050.SZ): the \$685M Optimus actuator-module order marks the component→module value migration; Tuopu (601689.SS) same pattern, with the customer-concentration risk that implies.

Monitors: harmonic/screw lead-time vs ASP divergence (squeeze confirming); Optimus 2026 landing in the 50K–150K range — the supply chain's master switch.

6. The corpus shapes the body; transparency is a data moat → hands & the QDD complex

Two original couplings, both investable:

  • Transparency premium — if transparent joints (QDD, tension-sensed tendons) emit force labels for free, fleets built on them compound a data moat. Beneficiaries: high-pole-count frameless motor makers (Kollmorgen under Regal Rexnord (RRX); Moons' (603728.SS)), joint-torque-sensor content, and — via magnet mass per joint — §5's magnet names again.
  • Anthropomorphic hand complex — if five-finger tendon hands win for data reasons, content shifts to coreless micromotors (AMETEK (AME) — global top-3; Maxon and Faulhaber private), micro-screws and tendon conduits, and hand drive modules (Zhaowei (003021.SZ)). Tactile appears here a second time (fingertip force is the on-robot force-grounding port) — the double-counted, highest-option-value category on the map.

Monitor: the DoF distribution of newly launched hands (five-finger tendon share rising = corpus-shapes-body confirming; two-finger grippers sufficing commercially = de-rate).

7. Autonomy's boundary = cheap verification → the verification layer

Self-scaling factories' first-order beneficiary is not the integrator — it is machine-vision QC, the merchant of "making the world a cheaper verifier."

  • Keyence (6861.T) and Cognex (CGNX) — price them as verifiability infrastructure, not cyclical industrials (an explicit divergence from consensus). Fanuc (6954.T) / Yaskawa (6506.T) are two-sided (displaced by humanoids vs selling into humanoid lines) — decompose by order book before owning the thesis through them.

Falsifier: end-to-end VLAs absorbing QC internally (the robot as its own judge) — itself a maturity reading on the world-model loop.

8. Integrators: the exception clause only

Default: squeezed (the Android-OEM script). Exception: fleet = data asset. Value integrators on force-labeled interaction hours per deployed robot per day × exclusivity, not on hardware gross margin.

  • Tesla (TSLA) — fleet + captive factory scenes + vertical actuators; the hand is the ramp bottleneck and thus the tell.
  • Unitree (Shanghai IPO — watch the listing) — shipments are data volume; the QDD-fleet transparency argument applies to them most directly.
  • UBTech (9880.HK), Xiaomi (1810.HK), XPeng (9868.HK) — optionality-weighted, China-deployment-economics beneficiaries.

Tell: the first integrator to disclose fleet-data KPIs (autonomous-hours curves, intervention rates) instead of unit shipments is the one accounting for the right asset.

10. Dashboard — quarterly, action-linked

  1. α (ego sample-efficiency exponent): strengthens → overweight §1–2; decays → rotate to fleet / real-data assets.
  2. World-model zero-shot on rigid contact: >~80% → pull every timeline in; the NVDA gym-rent thesis strengthens.
  3. Force-labeled data pricing emerges → the tactile double-count activates.
  4. Harmonic / screw lead-time vs ASP: divergence widens → §5 squeeze confirmed; converges → capacity caught up, trim.
  5. Nov 10, 2026: extension (gentle slope) vs tightening (steep) → adjust ex-China rare-earth weight; direction unchanged.
  6. New-design hand morphology: five-finger tendon share rising → §6 activates.
  7. Optimus hand yield / 50–150K landing — the supply chain's master switch.
  8. First fleet-data KPI disclosure by an integrator → the exception clause activates for that name.

Analysis, not investment advice. Tickers are exposure identifiers; no view on price or timing is expressed here — the Critique is, in large part, about why timing is the dominant risk to every line above.
